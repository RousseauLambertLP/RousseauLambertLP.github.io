## 1. ECMWF: the 9 km launch is postponed

**October 16 announcement · Importance: high for data consumers.** The ECMWF team now targets the week of October 19. It also clarifies that the bits-per-value reduction will affect the 0.25° data.

**Why it matters:** encoding changes can affect a pipeline even when its resolution remains unchanged.

**Suggested action:** retain existing settings until availability is confirmed and compare decoded values for threshold-sensitive variables.

**Source:** [ECMWF replies dated October 16](https://forum.ecmwf.int/t/ecmwf-free-and-open-data-at-9-km-soon-to-come/15366).

## 2. MeteoGate and WIS2: making data discoverable and usable

**WMO publication dated October 16 · Importance: high for interoperability.** WMO presents MeteoGate, developed by EUMETNET, as an interface for data discovery, preview and access. Its Data Explorer builds on the WIS2 Global Discovery Catalogue.

**Why it matters:** shared metadata and standards connect separate infrastructures. Practical access involves more than opening a file: users need to find a dataset and understand how to use it.

**Suggested action:** follow the discovery → preview → access journey and examine metadata quality and service links.

**Sources:** [WMO article](https://public.wmo.int/resources/meteoworld/meteoworld-october-2026/meteogate-opens-door-europes-weather-data) · [MeteoGate](https://meteogate.eu/).
