## Turning geospatial data into useful services

I’m a geospatial specialist at Environment and Climate Change Canada, within the Meteorological Service of Canada. My work focuses on developing, deploying and improving MSC GeoMet, the geospatial web services that provide access to Canadian weather, climate and water data.

I bring together geospatial expertise and software development to help make frequently updated datasets accessible through reliable services. That means working on the APIs and their data flows, resolving technical issues, and making sure the services meet the needs of the people and systems using them.

## Responsibilities and approach

My work combines implementation with coordination: translating requirements into practical improvements, contributing to deployment and maintenance, and working with colleagues to move services forward. I value teamwork, clear priorities and an agile approach to delivering useful changes.

Data standardization and governance are part of that work. Consistent metadata, clear conventions and open standards help make data easier to discover, interpret and integrate across organizations.

## Open data in practice

What interests me most is seeing data used beyond the platform where it was published. Open standards and well-designed APIs make that possible, but so do documentation, user feedback and collaboration between teams.

I share a [technology watch](/en/vigie/) on developments in open weather data, geospatial software and standards, with an emphasis on practical implications and original sources.
